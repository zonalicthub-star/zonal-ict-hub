About Us

The ICT Branch of the Wellawaya Zonal Education Office is dedicated to uplifting information technology standards in all regional schools.

Our Vision

"To create a digitally empowered student community in the Wellawaya educational zone through advanced ICT infrastructure and skilled educators."

Our Mission

"To facilitate continuous technical support, maintain accurate digital resource inventories, and elevate computer literacy standards across all zonal schools efficiently."
