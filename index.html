About Us

The ICT Division of the Wellawaya Zonal Education Office is dedicated to uplifting information technology standards in all regional schools.

Message from Assistant Director

"This website has been created to centralize and maintain information regarding Information and Communication Technology (ICT) teachers and students within the Wellawaya Education Zone. The data collected here will be used exclusively for the advancement of the ICT subject. I look forward to the continued support and collaboration of all our ICT teachers in keeping this platform updated. My sincere gratitude goes to Mr. K.M.R.P. Kelum and all the members in ZDETT Team (Wellawaya Zone Hardware and Network Solution Team) for their invaluable contribution to the creation of this site."
